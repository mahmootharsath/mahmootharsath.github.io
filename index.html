About me

Finance fundamentals, with an eye on how businesses actually run.

I completed my B.Com at SASTRA University, Thanjavur, and am now pursuing an MBA in Finance through distance learning at Madras University. My studies have given me a strong foundation in accounting, invoicing and financial transaction processing.

I first worked with these processes in practice as a Billing & Purchasing Executive at a trading firm, preparing invoices, recording purchases and following up on payments. That experience, together with hands-on exposure to Tally, Dolibarr and Max ERP, led me to study SAP, and I earned the SAP S/4HANA Cloud Private Edition Financial Accounting certification to understand how those same processes are structured in enterprise software.

I'm an analytical problem solver who values clear documentation, good communication and teamwork. I'm early in my career and treat learning as a habit, and I'm looking for SAP FICO, SAP Finance and finance operations roles where I can contribute carefully and keep building practical skills.
